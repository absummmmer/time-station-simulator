## Broadcast references

WWV/WWVH timing, tone schedule, BCD time code and UT1 correction behavior follow
public NIST documentation:

- https://www.nist.gov/pml/time-and-frequency-division/time-distribution/radio-station-wwv
- https://www.nist.gov/pml/time-and-frequency-division/time-distribution/radio-station-wwvh
- https://www.nist.gov/pml/time-and-frequency-division/time-distribution/radio-station-wwv/wwv-and-wwvh-digital-time-code
- NIST Special Publication 432, *NIST Time and Frequency Services*

BPM behavior follows the published BPM time-code format. The station operators
are not affiliated with this simulator.
